Horizon: three icon options

Each drawn from the board itself, in its own colors. Shown large, beside the wordmark, at app and tab sizes, and in a light and a dark browser tab.

1

Receding zones

The board's columns: Today first, tallest, and blue; This Week, Next Two Weeks, and Later shorter and quieter, all on one baseline. Position means time, in one glance. Warm paper ground, like the page.
