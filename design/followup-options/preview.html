A

The editor: Waiting, Hard deadline, Follow-up

Waiting is dashed with a plus when off, and a soft gray pill with an hourglass when on, never blue. On, it asks who or what you're waiting on (optional) and says since when; the day it was turned on is remembered, and turning it off forgets it.

Follow-up now takes the next card's title and, if you know it, its date. With both, Mark done makes the next card without asking. Leave either blank and you'll be asked then.

Three pills still don't fit in the editor's 420 pixels, so Follow-up wraps to its own line. The checklist is left out of these drawings to save room; it stays where it is.

B

On the board

Waiting shows in the card's meta row, where In progress was: an hourglass, who, then the days in mono, so it ages where you can see it. Set today, it says "today". A long name is cut short with an ellipsis so the days always show; the hover box and the editor have it whole. The hover box adds a line for each: since when you've waited, and what comes next.

Still yours to judge by eye: does "Follow-up" on a card read as this card has one waiting, or this card is one? If the second, the card's mark can say "Then…" while the editor's pill stays "Follow-up".

Mark done: a question only when something's missing

Title and date written ahead: no question. The editor closes as it does today, the next card is made, and the line beside quick add says so for a few seconds, with Edit, as it does after a quick add. Everything else's Mark done is exactly as now.

A title but no date: the editor asks when, one tap (Today, Tomorrow, and This week as in triage; This week is gone on a Sunday). Blank: it asks what, and the buttons wait for a title. The finished card is already saved, so nothing here can undo it. Nothing after all, the X, or Escape: a title you wrote goes to Someday rather than vanishing; a blank one is simply let go of.

What you did: the chain
