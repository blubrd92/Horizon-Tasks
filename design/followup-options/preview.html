Horizon: follow-ups

One look, drawn with the site's own styles: the Follow-up pill in the editor, the mark a card wears, the step after Mark done, and the chain in What you did. The sample cards are made up.

The pills work: click one, or Tab to it and press Space. Turning Follow-up on opens "What comes next?" below it.

A

The editor: a third pill

Follow-up sits beside In progress and Hard deadline: dashed with a plus when off, a quiet gray outline with an arrow when on. On, one line opens under it. Write the next task now, or leave it blank and you'll be asked when you mark it done. The checklist is left out of these drawings to save room; it stays where it is.

Three pills don't fit on one line in the editor's 420 pixels, so Follow-up wraps to a second line, on a phone and on a wide screen alike. Making the existing two smaller to squeeze it in would cost them their 44-pixel tap height, so I'd let it wrap.

A card that came from another says so at the top, in its own small box. That's the first place the chain shows.

B

On the board: the card wears it

An arrow and "Follow-up" in the card's meta row, the way Repeats shows its arrows and "Weekly". Resting the pointer on the card shows what comes next in the hover box, under the notes. The follow-up card itself looks like any other card: the chain stays off the board.

One thing to judge by eye: does "Follow-up" on a card read as this card has one waiting, or as this card is one? If the second, the card's mark can say "Then…" instead, and the pill in the editor stays "Follow-up".

C

After Mark done: one tap to place it

The finished card is saved before this step appears, so nothing here can undo it. The editor turns into one question: what comes next, and when? Each "when" button adds the card and closes, in one tap. Today, Tomorrow, and This week mean what they mean in triage (This week is the last day of the week; it's left out on a Sunday). Pick a date opens the date box. The new card keeps the category and project.

Nothing after all, the X, or Escape: a follow-up you wrote in advance goes to Someday rather than vanishing; a blank one is simply let go of. If it was blank, the buttons wait until there's a title.

D

What you did: the chain

In the list, a card that's part of a chain gets a small link mark after its title, like the repeat mark. Click the row, and its box shows the whole chain, oldest first, this card in bold: a filled check for finished, an open circle for still on the board, and "Let go" where you set one down.

Copy as text adds the chain only with the notes option ticked, as one "After:" line, so the plain list stays plain.
