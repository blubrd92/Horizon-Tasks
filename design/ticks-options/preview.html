Horizon: four looks for the ticks

In progress and Hard deadline, as they'd look in the card editor (drawn at its real width beside a card, 420 pixels) and in an Inbox draft. Each is shown off and on. Today they're two plain checkboxes.

Everything here works: click, tap, Tab to it and press Space. In look B, change the Due date to see the chip turn orange and back.

A

Switches

Like Settings' Fold long zones: a row each, the name and its note on the left, the switch on the right, in one small card. The safest choice: a control you already know, so Settings and the editor speak the same language.

A screen reader hears In progress, switch, off.

B

The card's own pill and chip, as buttons

My recommendation

Off: a dashed outline with a plus. On: exactly what the card will wear, the blue pill with its half circle, the Hard deadline chip with a check. The editor opens beside the card, so you see one set of marks in both places. The chip turns orange only when the Due date is today or past, as on the card (question 3 in PLAN.md: yes, or always gray here?).

A screen reader hears In progress, toggle button, pressed.

C

Two choices, both named

Status: Not started or In progress. Deadline: Soft or Hard. The most honest about what a card holds (every deadline is soft or hard; "soft" just never shows today). It borrows the look of Settings' tabs, which is its risk: it could read as tabs. The wordiest of the four.

A screen reader hears Deadline, Hard, radio button, selected, 2 of 2.

D

Two tiles, side by side

Each a small box with an icon, its name, and a line on what it does, and a check in the corner when on (the border turns blue too). The roomiest, and the only one that explains In progress. In an Inbox draft, and on a narrow phone, the tiles stack.

A screen reader hears In progress, checkbox, checked, then the line under it.
